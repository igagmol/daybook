Daybook – Privacy policy

Daybook is a personal, non-commercial app. It has no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What data is used

When you sign in with Google, Daybook reads your Google account's basic profile (name, email and picture) to show who is signed in. Your shopping list, meals, money entries, events, tasks and note are stored on your device.

Google Drive

Daybook uses the drive.file permission to keep a backup of your data in a folder called "Daily App" in your own Google Drive. With this permission the app can only see the files it created itself. Nobody else, including the app's author, can access your data.

Sharing

Your data is never sold or shared with anyone. It only travels between your device and your own Google account.

Deleting your data

You can delete the "Daily App" folder in your Google Drive at any time, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
